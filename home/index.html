END-TO-END WORKFLOW

From Voice to Action.

One intelligent workflow connecting citizens, AI, and government departments with zero friction.

01

Citizen Reports

Citizen submits text, vernacular voice note, geotagged image, and precise location through Web, WhatsApp, or IVR.

02

AI Understands

Bhashini NLP extracts spoken dialects, recognizes civic intent, validates photo authenticity, and tags key entities.

03

AI Prioritizes

Calculates multidimensional severity factoring in public health, school/hospital proximity, and hazard levels.

04

Complaint is automatically routed to the exact division officer and field maintenance crew in under 2 seconds.

05

SLA Monitoring

06

Field Resolution

Citizen receives instant photographic resolution proof, confirms closure, and provides feedback score.

Citizen Help & Support

1 How to Submit an Issue
Click Submit Issue, describe the problem using text or record a vernacular voice note (Hindi, Marathi, English). Attach a photo and verify your GPS location pin.
2 How to Track an Issue
Click Track Issue and enter your reference ID (e.g. LOK-2026-8941) received via SMS/WhatsApp. You can view the live status, assigned officer, and SLA countdown timer.
3 What Happens After Submission
LokVaani AI instantly analyzes your report, checks for duplicates, and auto-dispatches a digital work order to the exact municipal division and field crew in under 2 seconds.
4 How AI Prioritizes Complaints
Our dynamic AI severity matrix scores urgency (0-100) by evaluating public safety risks, proximity to schools or hospitals, water/power critical assets, and citizen density.
5 What SLA Means
SLA (Service Level Agreement) is the guaranteed resolution timeframe (e.g. 4 hours for hazardous water bursts, 24 hours for roads). If an SLA clock expires, the issue automatically escalates to the Chief Municipal Officer (CMO).
